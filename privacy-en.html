Privacy Policy

PriceAlerts — Last updated: November 22, 2026

Summary: no account is required, no personal identity data is collected, no ad or analytics SDK is integrated. Your watchlist and price alerts stay on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal identity information. The app does not request your phone number, real name, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location.

If you choose to use the in-app account feature (email and password), we store only: your email address, the password you set, registration and last login timestamps, and a random installation identifier bound to the account.

2. Data Stored On Your Device

Watchlist, price alert conditions and history, and display preferences are stored locally and removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Market Data

Prices, percentage changes, market cap rankings and charts come from public third-party market data APIs. Requests carry only public parameters such as coin symbols.

4. Notifications

Price alerts use iOS local notifications. You may disable them at any time in Settings > Notifications. Notification content is generated on device.

5. Third Parties

No advertising platforms, behavioral analytics SDKs, social logins or marketing push services are integrated.

6. Security

Account credentials are transmitted over HTTPS and stored on our server solely for account verification. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed absolutely secure. If you prefer, you may skip the account feature entirely; all market and alert features work offline.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

8. Your Rights

You may view your account email in the Profile tab, log out at any time, request deletion of your account by email, or uninstall the app to clear all local data.

9. Disclaimer

The app is a market display and alert tool. It does not provide trading, deposits, withdrawals, wallet custody or token issuance, and nothing in it constitutes investment advice. Market data may be delayed.

10. Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected by updating the date above.
